# IEEPA Tariffs Struck Down & How to Get Refunds (CAPE)

> As of **2026-09-16**. Tariff policy changes frequently — see the [Tariff Radar (change log + email alerts)](https://invoicetariff.com/en/radar) for the latest.
> 其他语言：[中文](../zh/03-ieepa-tariffs-and-refunds.md)

## What the IEEPA tariffs were

In 2025 the U.S. government imposed two rounds of tariffs under the **International Emergency Economic Powers Act (IEEPA)**:

- **"Fentanyl" tariffs** — imposed on specific countries citing the fentanyl issue;
- **"Reciprocal" tariffs** — country-by-country tiers imposed on trading partners.

Both were reported at entry through Chapter 99 headings **9903.01 / 9903.02**, stacking on top of MFN, Section 301 and the other layers.

## 2026-02-24: the Supreme Court strikes them down

**On February 24, 2026, the U.S. Supreme Court ruled the IEEPA tariffs unlawful** (consolidated opinion). Since then:

- New entries **no longer** carry the 9903.01 / 9903.02 layer — that stack is gone from new entries;
- **Everything outside IEEPA is unaffected**: the China Section 301 lists (Lists 1–4A), the [global Section 301 program](05-global-section-301.md) effective 2026-07-24, Section 232, and AD/CVD are all still collected.

Common misreading: "the tariffs were struck down = nothing is owed anymore." Only the IEEPA layer fell. Many goods still carry MFN + 301 + 232 — run the numbers before celebrating.

## Who can claim a refund

Duties paid under the IEEPA headings (9903.01 / 9903.02) **between 2025-02-04 and 2026-02-24** are refundable through the **CBP CAPE process**.

Key points:

- **Only the IEEPA layer is refunded.** The MFN, 301, 232, AD/CVD amounts on the same entry, plus MPF/HMF fees, are **not** refundable.
- Whether headings 9903.01 / 9903.02 were reported at entry is the direct evidence that IEEPA duty was paid — pull your entry summaries (CBP Form 7501) and check line by line.
- The **importer of record** on the formal entry is the claimant; if a broker files for you, the broker initiates and tracks the claim.

## Steps

1. **Scope the window**: review only entries filed between 2025-02-04 and 2026-02-24.
2. **Find the IEEPA lines**: on each 7501, locate headings 9903.01 / 9903.02 and the duty deposited against them.
3. **File through CAPE**: submit entry documents and proof of payment per CBP requirements; if a broker handles it, have them initiate and track each claim.
4. **Keep records and watch deadlines**: refunds are finalized case by case — log claim dates and amounts per entry and follow through to payment.
