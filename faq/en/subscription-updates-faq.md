# Subscription & Updates FAQ (Tariff Radar / Waitlist / API)

> As of **2026-09-16**. The [Tariff Radar page](https://invoicetariff.com/en/radar) is authoritative.
> 其他语言：[中文](../zh/subscription-updates-faq.md)

Tariff rules change almost monthly: 301 exclusion windows, global 301 tiers, new Section 232 sectors, annual MPF adjustments… This page explains how to **subscribe to change alerts**, follow the change log, and get higher limits and API access.

## Tariff Radar

**Q: What is the Tariff Radar?**
The [Tariff Radar](https://invoicetariff.com/en/radar) is the site's tariff change tracker: it **scans official sources every 12 hours** (Federal Register, CBP, USTR, …) and publishes incremental entries in the [change log](https://invoicetariff.com/en/radar/changelog) the moment a change is detected — each entry carries its legal source (e.g. FR citation) and stays in sync with the ruleset powering the calculator.

**Q: Can I subscribe by email? How do I unsubscribe?**
Yes. Enter your email in the subscription card at the top of the [radar page](https://invoicetariff.com/en/radar) to receive change alerts; every alert carries an unsubscribe link at the bottom.

**Q: Is it free? What happens to my email?**
Free. The address is used only to send change alerts (consistent with the [privacy policy](https://invoicetariff.com/en/privacy): no tracking cookies, no ad profiling).

**Q: Why should I care about these changes?**
Duty follows the **entry date**: the global 301 took effect 2026-07-24, Section 232 pharmaceutical tariffs begin 2026-09-29, MPF min/max change 2026-10-01, and the 178 China exclusions expire **2026-11-10** — each one moves the landed cost of your next shipment (background: [global 301](../../policy/en/05-global-section-301.md), [China 301](../../policy/en/04-section-301-china.md)).

## Waitlist and higher limits

**Q: I hit the daily batch limit — now what?**
The [batch calculator](https://invoicetariff.com/en/batch) allows 200 rows per file and 5 files per day (browser-local counting). Need more? Leave your email on the batch page or the [API waitlist page](https://invoicetariff.com/en/api-waitlist) and we will open higher limits in order.

**Q: Is the waitlist the same list as the radar subscription?**
It is the same waitlist system, but topics are separate: subscribing on the radar page means "tariff change alerts", while the API waitlist page registers interest in "higher batch limits / API". They don't mix, and both can be unsubscribed at any time.

## API

**Q: Will there be an API?**
Planned. Demand from ERPs and SaaS that want tariff calculation embedded in their own workflows is being collected on the waitlist — register your use case and volume on the [API waitlist page](https://invoicetariff.com/en/api-waitlist) and you will be notified in order when it launches.

**Q: I'd rather not subscribe — how do I track changes myself?**
Bookmark the [change log](https://invoicetariff.com/en/radar/changelog) and check back regularly, or use the RSS feed the radar provides. For the rule framework, start from the policy articles in this repository: [how U.S. tariffs are calculated](../../policy/en/01-us-tariff-calculation.md).
