## Editorial conventions

- Every article carries an **"as of" date**. Tariff policy changes frequently; when a rate or date matters, we cite the **Federal Register notice** behind it. The live change log is on the [Tariff Radar](https://invoicetariff.com/en/radar).
- Chinese and English versions are **mirrored 1:1** — same structure, same numbers. Each file cross-links its counterpart.
- Rates and dates are kept consistent with the ruleset that powers [invoicetariff.com](https://invoicetariff.com), where every duty row links to its source notice.

## Disclaimer

These documents are general policy explainers for reference only — **not** customs, legal, or tax advice. Entry decisions should be based on the HTSUS, Federal Register notices, and CBP guidance, or confirmed with your licensed customs broker.
